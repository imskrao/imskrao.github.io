About

Nine years of shipping, and the habits that came with it.

I’m a Senior Software Engineer based in Gurugram, India. I care about the unglamorous parts - how a codebase ages, how fast it loads on a bad network, and whether the next engineer can understand it without a meeting.

I started as an intern writing jQuery for an ed-tech product in 2017 and have been building for the browser ever since.

Most of that time has been in React and TypeScript - design systems, dashboards, and long-lived internal tools where the interesting problems are never the components themselves. They’re the state that leaks between screens, the bundle that grew 40 kB a sprint while nobody watched, and the patterns the team copies because they were the first thing they saw.

So that’s where I spend my effort: setting the defaults early, writing the decision down, and reviewing code in a way that makes the next person better rather than just approved. I’ve grown from writing features to owning how they’re built - and I still prefer to have my hands in the code.

Away from the editor I read about systems design, break things in the devtools performance panel for fun, and rebuild this site more often than is strictly necessary.
